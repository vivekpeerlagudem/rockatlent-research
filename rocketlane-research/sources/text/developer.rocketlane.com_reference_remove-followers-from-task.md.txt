# Remove followers from a task by Id

Based on a task's unique identifier (`taskId`), users can remove followers associated with that task through the *Remove followers from a task by Id* API.

This endpoint is particularly useful for removing followers from a task.

> In case, any issues arise or further assistance is required, please refer to the [error handling](https://developer.rocketlane.com/docs/http-status-codes) section or reach out to our support team at <care@rocketlane.com> for a prompt resolution.
