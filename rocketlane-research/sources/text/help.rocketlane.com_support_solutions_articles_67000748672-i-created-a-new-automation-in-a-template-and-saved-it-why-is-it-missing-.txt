A: The reason your saved automation is not appearing is that it was created but not fully saved within the template context.

To ensure your new automation is available across other tasks and projects, you must explicitly save the automation in the template after you have created and before saving the the template it. Once the template is saved, the automation will be correctly registered and accessible for selection on new tasks.
