A: If a project isn't appearing for you in the Resource Management tab, even though others can see it, the most common reason is a specific view setting. Check if the "only show projects for visible dates" checkbox is enabled in your Resource Management view settings. This setting filters out projects that don't fall within your currently selected date range, which can inadvertently hide projects that are ongoing or have no specific tasks with effort allocations. Unchecking this option typically resolves the issue, allowing you to see all relevant projects.
