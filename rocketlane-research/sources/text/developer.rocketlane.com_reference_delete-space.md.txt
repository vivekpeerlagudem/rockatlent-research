# Delete space by Id

The *Delete space by Id* API allows users to remove a specific space from the system based on its unique identifier (`spaceId`). This endpoint provides a method for developers to permanently delete a space from the project when it is no longer needed or has become obsolete. Upon successful deletion, *`HTTP status 204`- no content* is returned as a response with no response body.

> 🚧 Warning
>
> Once the space is deleted, there's no option to retrieve it back.

***

> In case any issues arise or further assistance is required, please refer to the [error handling](https://developer.rocketlane.com/docs/http-status-codes) section or reach out to our support team at <care@rocketlane.com> for a prompt resolution.
