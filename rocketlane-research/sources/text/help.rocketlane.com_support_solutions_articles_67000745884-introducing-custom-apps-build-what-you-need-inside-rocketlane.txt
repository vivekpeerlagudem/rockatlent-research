Introducing Custom Apps: Build What You Need Inside Rocketlane

No article available

Rocketlane now supports Custom Apps , enabling you to tailor and extend your workspace by embedding apps that mirror your internal processes. Whether it’s auto-carrying forward tasks, unifying data from multiple systems, or bringing in workflows that reflect how your teams operate—Custom Apps make it possible.
No more context switching. No more scattered tools. You can now bring everything you need right inside Rocketlane.

What Can You Do with Custom Apps?
Custom Apps let you:

Build custom workflows inside Rocketlane
From onboarding flows to customer success automations, define your own rules and UI.

Unify data from other tools
Pull and display data from Salesforce, ERPs, and more to create a centralized view of everything your team needs.

Surface key insights and actions where it matters
Embed your app in strategic locations across the platform to reduce toggling and improve efficiency.

Where Can Custom Apps Live?
You can choose where to display your app based on the use case:
Location Ideal Use Case
Customer Portal Widget Show key data or actions for customers in their view.
Project Tab Embed workflows or dashboards at a project level.
Account Tab Display apps that reflect account-wide data or tools.
Instance-wide Page Host utilities or reports accessible across Rocketlane.

How to Upload a Custom App
Once you've built your app, installing it on Rocketlane is simple.

Step-by-Step:

Build your app in your preferred code editor.

Refer to the Rocketlane Developer Documentation for app structure, sample apps, and integration details.

Go to Settings > Custom Apps .

Click Create App .

Name your app and upload the app file (ZIP).

Click Install .

✅ Once installed, your app will appear in the designated section (e.g., Project tab, Account tab).
