# Remove members from a project

Team members are participants from your company who work on projects and deliverables. Using this API endpoint, you can remove specific team members from a project using the `projectId`. The API returns the updated project object, which includes details of the remaining team members and their roles.

***

> In case any issues arise or further assistance is required, please refer to the [error handling](https://developer.rocketlane.com/docs/http-status-codes) section or reach out to our support team at <care@rocketlane.com> for a prompt resolution.
