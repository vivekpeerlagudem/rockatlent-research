Q: Why didn't the bot record my meeting, and why can't I find the recording in my account?

No article available

A: The bot was unable to record your meeting due to a lack of recording permissions. Additionally, the recording was not synced because our system is configured to only pull meetings where an active team member is the host. Since the host of your meeting was an external user, the meeting was not synced. Please note that it is not possible to change the host of a completed Zoom meeting.
