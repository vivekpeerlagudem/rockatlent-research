You can embed certain file types to Spaces. This makes it easier for you and your team to view files and documents that are related to the project without having to go to another platform.

How to Embed a File in Spaces

Go to the Spaces tab of your project and add a file to any of the Private or Shared spaces.

Click Embed URL .

The View your files by adding a link from apps and services dialog box mentions the list of supported links.

Add the URL to the file that needs to be embedded.

Click Embed .

When embedding files, make sure:
Links are publicly shareable.

Links are iFrame-compatible. You may need to confirm this with your file-sharing service.

For Dropbox, adjust the link by replacing dl=0 with raw=1 for embedding. Rocketlane uses Iframely, which may unfurl certain URLs.

You can now view your file from your project's Spaces .
