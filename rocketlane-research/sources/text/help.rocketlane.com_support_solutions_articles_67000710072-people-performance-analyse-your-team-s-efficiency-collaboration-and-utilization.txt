The People Performance Report lets you analyse the performance of your team members. It also has sections listing the top performers based on task completion, billable utilization and collaboration.

You can view each team member in the account and track their performance based on their information for the respective fields.

Filter

Lets you filter team members based on roles and placeholders or even exact team member names.

Timeframe

This filter lets you analyse the people performance based on time frames so that you can specifically monitor performances for specific time periods.

The ' Custom' option lets you add custom ' Start date' and ' Due date' so that you can analyse people performance for specific dates.

Top Performer Cards

Top Performers: Task Completion
The Task completion top performers card lists the top three team members based on the percentage of total tasks marked as Completed.

Top Performers: Billable Utilisation
The Billable Utilization top performers card lists the top three team members based on the percentage of total hours marked as Billable out of the Available capacity (Capacity − Time off − Holidays) of a team member.

Top Performers: Collaboration
The Collaboration top performers card lists the top three team members based on the total number of messages and comments sent.

The People Performance Report Table

Here is a list of the People Performance table fields:

Team Members

Lists all the team members added to the Rocketlane account.

Role

Mentions the Role that the team member has been added as.

Total Projects

Mentions the total number of Projects the team member has been assigned to.

Tasks Assigned

Mentions the new Tasks assigned to the team member in the selected timeframe.

Total Tasks

Mentions the total Tasks assigned to the team member in the selected timeframe.

Tasks Completed on-time

Mentions the total Tasks assigned to the team member that have been marked as complete before the Due date.

Tasks Completed late

Mentions the total Tasks assigned to the team member that have been marked as complete after the Due date.

Tasks Blocked

Mentions the total Tasks assigned to the team member that have been marked as Blocked at least once.

Tasks marked At- risk

Mentions the total Tasks assigned to the team member that have been marked as At- Risk at least once.

Messages and Comments

Mentions the total number of messages and comments sent by a team member.

Capacity

Mentions the total capacity of the team member for the selected time frame in terms of hours.

Hours Tracked

Mentions the total number of hours that have been tracked by the respective team member from the total budgeted hours (both billable and non billable).

There is a clickable link here which takes you to the time tracking report of that team member for better visibility of the hours tracked by them.

Billable Utilisation:

Mentions the Billable Utilisation of team members based on the percentage of total hours marked as Billable out of their Available capacity (Capacity − Time off − Holidays).

Billable utilization = (Billable time / (Capacity − Time off − Holidays)) * 100
