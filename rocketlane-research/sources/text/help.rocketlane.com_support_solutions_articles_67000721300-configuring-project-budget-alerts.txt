Budget alerts notify the relevant people when a project reaches a specified percentage of its budget or budgeted hours. You can create multiple alerts to track budget usage at different stages of a project.
Budget alerts are available when Financial Management is enabled. To learn more about managing project finances, see the Financial Management course in Rocketlane Academy .

Configure project budget alerts

Click your profile icon, then click Settings .

Under Operations and financials , click Financial management .

Make sure Enable financial management is turned on. Budget alerts are unavailable until it is.

Under Project budget alerts , next to Notify via: , select Email , In-App , or Slack . To use Slack, connect the Slack integration first.

In the BUDGET ALERT table, click New alert .

In Notify when the threshold is breached , enter a percentage, for example, 50.

Project owners are always notified through Notify project owners . To also alert the project team, select Notify project team members . To add other users, choose them under Notify others .

Click Create .

Note Create several alerts, for example at 50%, 75%, and 90%, to track usage at different stages of the project.

Configure budgeted hours alerts
To alert on budgeted hours, click New alert in the BUDGETED HOURS ALERT table and follow steps 6 to 8 above. The pane is titled New project hour alert .

Edit or delete an alert
To change or remove an alert, hover over it and click the edit > delete icon.
