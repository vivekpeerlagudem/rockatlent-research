Setting Up Your Company Knowledge Base

No article available

Rocketlane AI relies on business context to interpret what counts as risk, opportunity, or operational importance for your organization. This context can be sourced from a knowledge base.

Create a knowledge base

Navigate to Settings → Knowledge base .

Select Create knowledge base .

Enter a name.

Assign permissions or roles. Permissions control who can update the knowledge base.

Select the type of data to add:
AI pages (information derived from transcripts)

API docs

Website

Upload a file from your computer

Select Add to the knowledge base to add the content.
