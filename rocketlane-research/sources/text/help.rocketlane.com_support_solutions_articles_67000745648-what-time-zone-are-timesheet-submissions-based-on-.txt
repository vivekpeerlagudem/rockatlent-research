Q: Are timesheet submission reminders based on the user's local timezone or the account's default timezone?
A: Currently, timesheet submission reminders are sent based on the account's default timezone , not the individual user's local timezone.
For example, if your Rocketlane account is set to the US Eastern Time Zone and a reminder is scheduled for 6:00 PM EST, all users will receive that reminder at 6:00 PM EST, regardless of their physical location (e.g., a user in the UK would receive it in their early morning hours).
