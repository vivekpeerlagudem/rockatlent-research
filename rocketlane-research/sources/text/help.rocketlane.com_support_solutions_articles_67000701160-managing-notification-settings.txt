Notifications help you stay updated about project-related activity. You can customise which notifications you receive for your user account and choose how you receive them.
Notification preferences are available for features such as tasks, messages, documents, forms, project updates, timesheets, and project dates.
You can also configure reminders for upcoming task start dates and due dates.

Accessing notification settings
You can access Notification Settings in two ways.

From Notifications

Click the bell icon in the left navigation bar.

Click the Configure icon from the Notifications view.

From Settings

Go to Settings from the left navigation bar.

Click Notifications .

The Notification Settings page lets you customise your notification preferences.

Notification types
Depending on the notification, you can receive updates through email, in-app notifications, or Slack.

Note To clear all notifications, click Clear All in the Notifications pane. To restore cleared notifications, open Cleared Notifications and click Restore .

Feature-based notification settings
Notification settings are grouped by feature. You can enable or disable the available notification channels based on the updates you want to receive.
The following tables show the available notifications and their default settings.
Note ✅ indicates a notification that is enabled by default. ☑️ indicates a notification that cannot be unsubscribed from.

Sheets
Sheets notifications keep you informed about changes to sheets, tabs, and row assignments in projects you belong to.
Notification Email In-app Slack
Sheet deleted ✅

Sheet tab status changed ✅ ✅
Sheet tab is assigned to me ✅ ✅ ✅
Sheet tab assignee changed ✅
Sheet tab due date changed ✅ ✅
Sheet tab name changed ✅
Sheet tab deleted ✅
Sheet row is assigned to me ✅ ✅ ✅
Note Creating a sheet or changing the sheet's own status, assignee, or due date is covered by the Tasks notification settings. The Sheets section covers sheet deletion and changes to tabs and rows. Comments and mentions inside sheet cells are controlled under Messages , not Sheets . Slack checkboxes remain unavailable until the Slack integration is configured. Sheets notifications are delivered as Slack direct messages and cannot currently be sent to Slack channels.

The two assigned to me events notify only the person being assigned. All other Sheets events notify project members except the person who made the change. You receive a notification only if you are a project member and have permission to view the sheet. None of the Sheets notifications are mandatory.

Timesheets
Timesheet notifications keep submitters informed about reminders and approval outcomes, while notifying approvers when a timesheet requires review. Email and in-app notifications are enabled by default. You can turn off individual notifications that you do not want to receive.

Timesheet submission notifications
These notifications are sent to the person submitting the timesheet.
Notification When it is sent Email In-app
Timesheet submission reminder Before the timesheet due date ✅ ✅
Timesheet overdue reminder After the due date if the timesheet has not been submitted ✅ ✅
Timesheet approved When an approver approves the timesheet ✅ ✅
Timesheet rejected When an approver rejects the timesheet ✅ ✅

Timesheet approval notifications
These notifications are sent to timesheet approvers.
Notification When it is sent Email In-app
Timesheet approval requested When a timesheet is submitted for the approver's review ✅ ✅
Note The due date and timing of submission and overdue reminders are configured separately by an admin under Settings > Time Tracking . Your notification preferences determine whether you receive those reminders but do not change when they are scheduled.

Note Turning off a timesheet notification affects only your own notification preferences. It does not change the timesheet submission or approval workflow for other users.
