The Rocketlane Spaces feature lets you share content related to your project with your team members. You can draft shared documents, mark documents as private, embed URLs for shareable files, and use predesigned document templates.

How to access Spaces

Go to Projects and choose any project.

Click the Spaces tab.

The view for a blank Spaces tab looks like this. You can now start adding content to your Spaces.

Spaces: Features and Functionalities

Search Space Documents Search Bar
Allows you to look up a specific shared or private document.

Private and Shared Spaces
To read more about private and shared spaces and how to create spaces, click here .
Space type Description
Private Spaces Allows you to privately share documents within your team without involving the customer's team.
Shared Spaces Allows you to share documents with your team and the customer's team.

Blank Document
Lets you get started on a blank document from your Rocketlane project.
To read more about creating a Rocketlane document, click here .

Embed URL
View your files by adding links from apps and services.
To read more about embedding a file in Spaces, click here .

Use a Template
Allows you to choose an existing document template for your document.
To know more about document templates, click here .

Rocketlane Documents
Creates collaborative private and shared documents accessible to team members of the project.
