The Customer Portal helps customers stay connected with their projects. Customers can view project progress, access relevant tasks and documents, communicate with your team, receive notifications, and use self-serve resources such as FAQs and training content.
You can also customize the Customer Portal to match your brand and decide what pages, sections, widgets, and content should be shown to customers.

Create a Customer Portal template
You can create a Customer Portal template from the Templates section and customize its theme, colors, buttons, and fonts.

Go to Templates and open the Customer Portal Templates section.

Click New Customer Portal Template .

Add your website URL so Rocketlane can suggest a theme for your portal.

To edit colors, go to the Colors section, click the + icon, and add your preferred color to the palette.

To edit buttons, click Edit near the button style and customize the primary, secondary, and tertiary buttons.

To edit font styles, click Edit near the style option and customize the font name, size, and style for headings and paragraph text.
Button settings include:

Shape: Choose the button shape.

Text color: Choose the color of the button text.

Button color: Choose the button background color.

Outline: Choose the border color and thickness.

Font: Choose the font and size used on the button.

Create a theme from Settings

Go to Settings and scroll to the Customer Portal section.

Open Customer Portal Theme , click New Customer Portal Theme , and follow the same theme setup steps.

Configure typography in a Customer Portal theme
Typography settings determine how text appears throughout Customer Portals that use the theme. Each setting represents a global typography role rather than a specific portal page, section, or widget.
When you update a role, Rocketlane applies the selected font family, weight, and size everywhere that role is used. For example, changing Heading 2 updates every Heading 2 element across Customer Portals using that theme.
To configure typography:

Open the relevant Customer Portal Theme .

Go to the typography or font styles section.

Click Edit next to the typography role you want to customize.

Select the font family, weight, and size.

Save your changes.

Preview the portal on desktop, tablet, and mobile to review the updated typography.

Typography roles
Typography role Used for
Heading 1 Largest display text, including hero titles and large metric or value displays.
Heading 2 Major section headings and prominent widget titles.
Heading 3 Section headings and widget titles.
Heading 4 Smaller headings and subsection titles.
Paragraph Large Larger body text and descriptive content.
Paragraph Medium Standard body text, field values, and most descriptive content.
Paragraph Small Smaller body text and supporting information.
Heading 5, Heading 6, and Heading 7 Reserved typography roles that are not currently used by live Customer Portal components.
Link Hyperlink styling that is not currently used by live Customer Portal components.
Caption Helper text and metadata styling that is not currently used by live Customer Portal components.
Quote Block quote styling that is not currently used by live Customer Portal components.

Note Button text is not controlled by the typography settings. Configure button typography and appearance separately from the Buttons section of the theme editor.

Responsive typography behavior
On tablets and mobile devices, Rocketlane adjusts configured heading and paragraph font sizes to maintain a consistent responsive experience. The selected font family continues to apply across device sizes.
If you do not configure a font for a typography role, Rocketlane uses the following default font stack:
SF Pro Display, Segoe UI, Arial

Navigation bar
The navigation bar helps customers move between pages in the Customer Portal. Each page you add to the portal is represented by an icon in the navigation bar.

Default pages in the navigation bar
Rocketlane includes default pages that support customer visibility, communication, and collaboration. These pages cannot be disabled or deleted while customizing the portal.

Accounts Overview
The Accounts Overview page gives customers a complete view of account health, engagements, and projects. Customers can track project progress, review documents, and understand their overall relationship with your team.

Chat page
The Chat page lets customers communicate with team members and collaborate on project-related tasks.

Notifications
Notifications keep customers informed about project and account updates, such as task assignments, document mentions, and other activity. Customers can view notifications from the bell icon in the Customer Portal.

Action items
Action items help customers find items that need their attention, such as pending approvals and customer surveys triggered when milestone tasks are completed.

Project Switcher
If a customer is part of multiple projects, the Project Switcher lets them move between projects and work on project-specific tasks.

Build your portal
Before building a portal, decide what information customers need and how it should be organized. You can create pages for project plans, FAQs, training resources, account reviews, and more.
A good portal structure usually includes:

Required pages: Decide which pages the portal should include, such as a home page, project plan page, FAQ page, or training page.

Sections: Decide what each page should contain. For example, the home page can include a welcome message, task list, resources, and footer.

Widgets: Add widgets such as task lists, FAQs, team members, forms, or project updates to make each section useful.

Pages
Pages help organize the Customer Portal into clear areas, such as Home, Project Plan, FAQs, Training, or Account Review. Customers can navigate these pages like a website.

Sections
Sections help divide each page into focused blocks of content. For example, a page can include a hero section, project tasks, resources, training videos, FAQs, and a footer.

Create pages and add sections

Create the page

Go to Templates and select the Customer Portal template.

Click Create New Portal .

Name the portal.

Click the + icon on the left to create a new page.

Name the page, such as Home Page .

Click Add Sections to start building the page.

Add sections

Open the page you want to edit.

Click the + icon to create a new section.

Choose a widget from the list of available widgets.

Add a title and description for the section.

Use SmartFill where needed to personalize content, such as customer names.

Add conditions if the section should only show specific data.

Style sections
You can style sections to match your brand and make the portal easier to read.

Customize background colors and spacing.

Add dividers and adjust their height or style.

Set dynamic conditions for Rocketlane sections to show relevant data.

Continue adding more sections, such as Features, Resources, and Footer.
Note You can add as many pages as needed to match your customers’ needs.

Available sections
Customer Portal sections are grouped into Rocketlane sections and static sections.

Rocketlane sections
Section Description
Project Plan Shows project phases and tasks. Customers can view progress, overdue tasks, spotlight tasks, and upcoming work.
Project Plan Board View Shows a phase-wise board view of the project flow, tasks, and assignees.
Project Plan Timeline View Shows project timelines using a Gantt chart format.
Forms Shows forms attached to the project. Customers can access, fill, submit, or edit forms where applicable.
Key Information Shows important project fields and summary information.
All Files Shows project-related files such as contracts, agreements, and other documents.
Spaces Provides access to project documents such as scope documents and SOWs.
Team Members Shows all project team members in one place.
Project Updates Shows project progress updates that are shared with customers.
Task List Shows project tasks, statuses, deadlines, task views, and ownership details.
Project Overview Shows project progress, milestones, risks, team members, and updates.
Project Hours Shows hours allocated and tracked on the project.

Static sections
Section Description
FAQ Adds frequently asked questions for customers to self-serve.
Hero Section Adds a prominent top section that highlights key information or offerings.
Logos Displays logos or icons for partnerships and collaborations.
Metrics Displays company or project statistics.
Feature Highlights key features of your product or service.
Image Gallery Displays project-related images.
Resources Links to external resources such as websites, help centers, or landing pages.
Iframe Embeds videos from YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Wistia, or another video provider using a video URL or HTML iframe embed snippet.

Note You can move sections up or down, duplicate them, and delete sections that are no longer needed.

Note Rocketlane sections are dynamic and populate data based on project information.

Configure a section
Each section includes configuration options based on the section type.
Field What it does Used in
Title Adds a title to the section. Most static sections
Description Adds supporting text for the section. Most static sections
Image Adds an image to visually support the section. Hero, Feature, Resources, Videos, Image Gallery
Metrics Adds numbers or statistics. Metrics
Buttons and CTAs Adds buttons or links that redirect customers to another page or resource. Feature, Resources, CTA, Videos
Question and Answer Adds FAQ content. FAQ
Embed code Accepts a video URL or HTML iframe embed snippet. Iframe
Group Title and Link Name Organizes footer links into groups. Footer, Resources
Item tabs Organizes multiple items, each with its own embed code. Tabs can be added, selected, or deleted. Iframe
Styling Controls background color, spacing, dividers, divider height, and flip style. All sections

Embed videos with an Iframe section
Use the Iframe section to embed videos from YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Wistia, or another video provider into the Customer Portal. Alternatively you can use the Videos widget to embed simple Youtube links

Open Customer Portal Templates , select the template you want to edit, and open the required page.

Hover over the boundary where you want the video to appear and click the + button.

Under Static sections , select Iframe and choose a layout.
[image: Iframe layouts in the Static sections drawer]
In the Section: Iframe sidebar, open Details .

Enter a title and description.

Select an item tab and paste the video URL or HTML iframe embed snippet into Embed code .

Add tabs for additional embeds, switch between tabs, or delete unused tabs as needed.

Review the embedded video section in the portal editor.

Click Done to apply the section configuration.

Click Preview to verify how the embedded video appears to customers.
Note To embed a Loom or Wistia video, convert the provider embed code to HTML iframe format and paste it into the Embed code field. Recommended iframe size: 600 px x 918 px .

Dynamically add pages to the Customer Portal
You can use conditional rules to decide when certain pages should appear in the Customer Portal.
For example, if you want the project chat page to appear only for projects with fees above a certain amount, you can add that condition. The page will then be excluded from portals that do not meet the condition.

Preview the Customer Portal
You can preview the Customer Portal while building the theme to see how it will appear to customers.

Go to Templates and select the Customer Portal Template .

Choose the template you want to preview.

Click Preview from the top-right corner.

Preview the portal in Mobile , Desktop , or Tablet mode.

Use the upward arrow to scroll through the portal in the selected view.

Click Edit Portal to return to editing mode.

Change the Customer Portal language
Customers can change the portal language from the language icon at the top-right corner of the Customer Portal. By default, the portal language is picked from the customer’s browser language preference.
