How does it work?

1) You don’t have to be a Rocketlane customer to participate in this referral programme.

2) Introduce Rocketlane to a friend, or a former colleague, or someone you know that can benefit from using our software, and point us to them by filling this form . Alternatively, scan the QR code below to share the referral with your friend and us in a jiffy!

3) Once you submit the form, we will reach out to them and showcase our product. You will receive regular updates about the status of your referral. If the referral is part of our existing discussions already, it will not be considered for this program.

4) If your referral ends up purchasing Rocketlane ( at least an equivalent of Standard Plan and 5 users on an annual commitment ) , your organisation gets $1000 of credits for your Rocketlane plan, and you receive a $350 gift voucher or a donation to a charity of your choice.

5) Offer is valid till 1st September 2024 .

If you have questions, email care@rocketlane.com
