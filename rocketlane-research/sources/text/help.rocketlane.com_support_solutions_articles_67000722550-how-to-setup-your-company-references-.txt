As your organization grows, Rocketlane needs to reflect how your team actually operates: which timezone you work in, how you measure capacity, when your fiscal year starts, and how your account looks to your own team and to your customers.
Account Preferences is where you configure these defaults for your entire Rocketlane account. Changes here apply account-wide, not just to your own user profile.

Where to find it
Go to Settings , then select Preferences under the Account section of the left-hand navigation.

Who can access these settings

Viewing this page requires the Can manage account preferences permission. Changing a setting requires the write-level version of the same permission. If a setting appears without a Change link or editable control, you have read-only access to it.

Partner users see only Company logo and Company name . The remaining settings, including Timezone, Weekly team capacity, Utilization, Organisation calendar, Appearance, and Sample data, are not shown to Partner users.
Note Sample data behaves differently from the other settings on this page. If you do not have write access, it is hidden entirely instead of appearing as read-only.

What you can configure
Setting What it controls
Company logo The logo shown across your account
Company name Your organization's display name
Timezone The timezone used for account-wide date and time calculations
Weekly team capacity Expected weekly working hours per team member
Utilization How utilization percentage is calculated
Organisation calendar Your fiscal year start month
Appearance Sidebar branding and project plan background
Sample data Whether your account still has Rocketlane's sample content

Company logo

Go to Settings > Preferences .

Click Upload logo .

Choose an image file from your device.

Rocketlane replaces the current logo as soon as the upload finishes.

Company name

Click Change next to Company name .

Enter your organization's name.

Save your changes.
Note The name field is required. You cannot save an empty company name.

Timezone

Click Change next to Timezone .

Search for and select your timezone. Each option shows its current UTC offset alongside the name.

Save your changes.

Weekly team capacity
Rocketlane uses this figure across Resource Management, Timesheets, and capacity reports, so it is important to set it accurately.

Click Change next to Weekly team capacity .

Enter the number of hours and minutes, if needed, that a team member is expected to be available each week.

Save your changes.
Note The value must divide evenly across your organization's working days. Otherwise, Rocketlane asks you to adjust it.

Utilization
This setting controls how Rocketlane calculates each person's utilization percentage across Resource Management, Timesheets, and reports.

Click Change next to Utilization .

Choose how utilization should be calculated.

Save your changes.
Option Calculated against What it measures
Total Capacity Weekly capacity only Expected working hours based on weekly capacity
Total Capacity Net Holidays Weekly capacity minus holidays Expected working hours minus holidays
Total Capacity Net Timeoffs Weekly capacity minus time off Expected working hours minus time off
Available Capacity Weekly capacity minus holidays and time off Expected working hours minus holidays and time off
Note If you have not configured this setting, Rocketlane calculates utilization using Available Capacity by default.

Organisation calendar
Your fiscal year start month determines how Rocketlane groups dates into quarters across quarter-based reports and analytics.

Click Change next to Organisation calendar .

Select the month your fiscal year begins in. Rocketlane shows a preview of how Q1 through Q4 map to calendar months.

Save your changes.

Note If you have not set a fiscal year start month, Rocketlane uses January by default.

Appearance
Appearance controls how your account navigation looks, with separate previews for your team's view and your customers' view.
Setting What it does
Sidebar background color Sets the side navigation background color. Rocketlane automatically adjusts the sidebar text and hover color to remain readable.
Project plan background Shows or hides the background image on the project plan view.

Click Change next to Appearance .

Set a sidebar background color and turn the project plan background on or off.

Review the previews for your team and your customers.

Save your changes.

Sample data
New Rocketlane accounts include sample projects, tasks, and other content so you can explore the product before adding your own data.

If the account still contains sample content, select Remove sample data when you are ready to work with your own projects.

If the sample content has already been removed, the button reads Reload sample data . Use it to bring the sample content back, for example, to test a feature using example data before using it on a real project.

Note Removing sample data is permanent. Rocketlane asks you to confirm before deleting it.

FAQ

Why can't I see some of these settings?
Most Account Preferences settings are hidden from Partner users, and all settings require the Can manage account preferences permission to view.

Why can I see a setting but not change it?
You have read access but not write access to Account Preferences. Ask an account admin to update the setting or grant you write access.

Does changing the fiscal year start affect reports I have already built?
It changes how Rocketlane groups dates into quarters going forward in any quarter-based report or analytics view.
