Sprint reports provide valuable insights into the velocity, efforts, and hours of sprints by tracking the completion of sprint points and tasks. This information helps in predicting how many similarly complex tasks can be handled in future sprints.

Velocity chart:
This chart shows the comparison between the total points allocated to tasks and the total points completed based on completed tasks. It helps in determining the velocity of each sprint and understanding if a balanced workload is allocated for each sprint.

X-axis: Sprints
Y-axis: Task count/points

Planned vs actuals for sprints:

This comparison shows the sum of efforts allocated versus the hours tracked for the tasks in the sprints.
X-axis: Sprints
Y-axis: Sum of efforts/Tracked hours

Summary table:

The summary table provides a list of sprints, and clicking on each sprint gives an overview of the sprint.

The report tab gives you an overview of specific sprints for the selected time period
