Reactivating a deactivated user in Rocketlane is a straightforward process that does not involve any additional costs. Here is everything you need to know:

Step-by-Step Guide to Reactivating a User

Navigate to Settings in your Rocketlane account.

Go to Team Members and click on the Deactivated tab.

Locate the user you want to reactivate.

Click the three dots (•••) next to the user's name.

Select Reactivate from the options provided.

You have successfully reactivated the user. The user will now have access to their Rocketlane account as before.

Financial Considerations
Reactivating a previously deactivated user in Rocketlane is completely free of charge. It does not consume any additional licenses or incur any fees.

Additional Notes

These steps apply universally to any deactivated user in Rocketlane.

Ensure you have the necessary admin permissions to access and reactivate team members.

If you encounter any issues or have further questions, feel free to reach out to Rocketlane support.
