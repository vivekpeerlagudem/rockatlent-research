Rocketlane provides a comprehensive Invoice List Page , offering a dashboard-like experience to track and manage your invoices efficiently. With an intuitive layout, you can easily scroll through all invoices and get a quick glance at critical details such as invoice amounts, payment statuses, and outstanding balances.
Key metrics available at a glance:

Total Invoiced : The total amount invoiced to date.

Total Paid : The total amount received from customers.

Total Outstanding : The total amount yet to be paid.

Current : Invoices that are due but not overdue.

Overdue : Invoices that have crossed their due date.

Custom Filters and Saved Views
Focus on specific subsets of invoices using filters. For example:

Filter invoices by status, such as "In Review" or "Approved" .

Narrow down results by customer account names.

Once configured, save these filtered views for quick and easy access in the future.
