Q: Why can't I manually add or find an "Actual Completion Date" field to sync with Salesforce, and what's its equivalent?
A: You won't find a field explicitly named "Actual Completion Date" to add manually for syncing. This is because it's a calculated field within Rocketlane and is automatically available for Salesforce integration. When setting up your Salesforce field sync, look for it under the name "Due Date Actual." This field represents the same data as "Actual Completion Date" and can be synced directly to your Salesforce instance.
