Why is "Import from my allocations" coming up blank on my timesheet?

No article available

If allocations fail to populate on your timesheet when using the " Import from my allocations " option, check the following:

Task Assignment Dates : Verify that your assigned tasks fall strictly within the date range/timeframe selected on your timesheet.

Resource Allocations : Ensure that explicit resource allocations are set up for your user profile under the Resource Management tab for those projects during the given week/month.

Cache & Reload : If assignments and allocations are properly set up but the screen remains blank, refresh your browser or reach out to support so the technical team (L2) can sync your timesheet allocations.
